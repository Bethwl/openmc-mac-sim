File ket qua openmc_export.xlsx se duoc tao ra voi day du 7 Sheet:
  - MAC: Bang ma tran he so suy giam khoi luong.
  - T_Transmittance: Ma tran do truyen qua, tu dong to do canh bao neu T < 2% hoac T > 90%.
  - R_Compton: Ty le R phan ung Compton chuan hoa phuc vu tinh Zeq.
  - Do_day_mau: Bang tra cuu be day mau da dung.
  - QA_Transmittance: Bang kiem dinh do tin cay so dem va sai so thong ke.
  - Thoi_gian_chay: Tong ket thoi gian tung job va toan bo qua trinh.
  - Benchmark_Table2: So sanh truc tiep va tinh sai so tuong doi RD% so voi Table 2 Paper 1 (XCOM va MCNPX).
